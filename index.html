Research

Four lines of work, each one moving from a theoretical result to something that runs on real infrastructure. The constellation alongside is the problem in one picture: sixteen symbols scattered by channel noise, resolving onto the decision lattice as the receiver acquires lock.

Semantic communication beyond bit fidelity

A domain-aware, knowledge-enhanced framework that transmits what a message means rather than reproducing every bit, with physical-layer channel adaptation and data-preserving reconstruction. This was my doctoral thesis and remains the spine of the work.

IEEE TNSE 2026, IEEE OJCOMS 2025, ICT Express 2024

O-RAN and closed-loop AI-RAN

Coverage and capacity optimisation inside an O-RAN digital twin, testing static tuning against learned control, and taken through the full safe-automation path: shadow mode, promotion gates and automatic rollback before anything touches a live cell. Built and taught on NVIDIA AODT and VIAVI AIRSG.

IEEE TNSM, submitted

Integrated sensing and communication

A provable Doppler-induced orthogonality-loss gap in semantic-ISAC, validated inside a network digital twin, together with the O-RAN observable the standard currently lacks to detect it. Current work extends this to intent-driven agentic decisions on a UAV-ISAC twin built over ray-traced cities, on a verify-rehearse-commit loop.

IEEE APWCS 2026, Seoul. IEEE TNSE, submitted

Learned resource orchestration

Hierarchical multi-resolution deep Q-networks for physical resource block allocation across B5G heterogeneous networks, combining traffic prediction with interference management. Five patent filings came out of this line.

IEEE TCCN, under review

Project demonstrations

Recorded walkthroughs of three systems. Each one is the demo that accompanies the paper named beside it. Nothing downloads until you press play.

Coverage and Capacity Optimization in an O-RAN Digital Twin

Intent-Driven Agentic Decisions on a UAV-ISAC Digital Twin

An operator states an intent in natural language; the system verifies it against the mission contract, rehearses it in a forked twin, and only then commits under a lock. Built over ray-traced cities in Sionna RT.
